Nails Hair Master

A booking app for a beauty lounge or clinic. Clients book a service, a specialist and a time in under a minute. The owner sees every booking live and WhatsApp reminders cut no-shows.

  • Book service, specialist and time slot
  • Optional deposit to stop no-shows
  • Automatic WhatsApp confirmations and reminders
  • Owner dashboard: today's schedule, revenue, busy hours
  • Full Arabic and English
  • One codebase for iPhone and Android
Try it: book an appointment, then open the Business tab to see it land in the owner's schedule.
