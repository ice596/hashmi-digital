Celebrating Life

A loyalty and order-ahead app for a cake shop. Customers pre-order cakes for pickup or delivery, collect stamps, and come back for every birthday. The bakery owns its customer list instead of renting it from delivery apps.

  • Order ahead with a pickup time
  • Digital stamp card, points and member tiers
  • Full Arabic and English (tap the ع button)
  • Apple Pay and card checkout
  • Offers by push and WhatsApp to bring regulars back
  • One codebase for iPhone and Android
Tap around. Everything on the phone works: order a cake, watch it get boxed, earn a stamp.
