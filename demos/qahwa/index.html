An order-ahead and loyalty app for Qahwa Club, a specialty café on Al Reem Island. Regulars order before they park, the bar gets a ticket, and the stamp card fills itself.

  1. 01
    Order ahead, pick a time. ASAP or a slot, counter or curbside.
  2. 02
    A stamp card that stamps itself. Nine drinks, the tenth is on the house.
  3. 03
    English and العربية. One tap, full right-to-left layout.
  4. 04
    Tickets land on the bar. No calling out names, no lost orders.

It works. Build a drink, place the order, and watch the ticket print at the bar on the right. Two drinks fill the card.
