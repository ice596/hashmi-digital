Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Hashmi Digital ("we", "us") builds websites, WhatsApp booking assistants and AI video ads for businesses in the UAE. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and what we do with it.

What we collect

  • Contact details you send us, such as your name, phone number, email and business name, when you message us on WhatsApp, email us or use the chat on our website.
  • Photos and files you share so we can make ads or websites for you.
  • WhatsApp messages sent to assistants we run, for us or for our clients, such as the service you pick, the date and time you book, and your WhatsApp name and number.
  • Basic website analytics, such as pages visited and device type, without identifying you personally.

How we use it

  • To reply to you and provide the service you asked for.
  • To make bookings, send confirmations and reminders through WhatsApp.
  • To improve our website and services.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use it for unrelated advertising.

Who we share it with

Only the providers needed to run the service: Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform), our hosting providers (such as Cloudflare and GitHub), and, for booking assistants, the business you are booking with. Each of them handles data under their own privacy terms.

How long we keep it

We keep information only as long as needed to provide the service or meet legal requirements, then delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us at any time to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you. You can also stop WhatsApp messages from us or an assistant we run by replying "stop".
