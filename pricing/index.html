questions about price.

Why are you cheaper than big agencies?

We use AI to do the slow parts, like drafting designs, writing first versions and making videos, and a person checks every piece before you see it. No big office, no account managers. You get agency quality for a fraction of the price, and you talk to the person building it.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Start with Get booked Starter, and when you're ready to move up, you only pay the difference. Add Get seen any month.

When do I pay?

Get booked: 50% to start and 50% at launch. Get seen: paid monthly in advance, cancel anytime. The prototype is free.

What do I pay every month?

Nothing to us, unless you choose a Get seen plan. You pay your own domain (about AED 50 a year) and, for WhatsApp booking, the platform fee (about AED 200 to 440 a month) and Meta's small per-message fees, paid to the providers in your name.

What if I don't like the prototype?

You walk away and owe nothing. That's the whole point of making it first.

Which part should I start with?

If customers can't find you or wait for replies, start with Get booked. If you're already busy but rarely post, start with Get seen. Not sure? Send one WhatsApp and we'll tell you.
