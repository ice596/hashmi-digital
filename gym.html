    07Questions أسئلة شائعة

    Good to know.

    I've never trained before. Is that a problem?

    No. About a third of new members have never lifted or boxed. Every class has scaled options, and your first two weeks follow a beginner track built by your coach.

    Are there women only classes?

    Yes. Strength, boxing and conditioning each run women only sessions every week, coached by Mariam and Lina, with a private changing area.

    Can I freeze my membership?

    Up to four weeks a year, free, from the APEX app. Most members use it for summer travel or Ramadan.

    What are the Ramadan hours?

    During Ramadan we open 13:00 to 17:00 and 21:00 to 02:00, with a reduced timetable and lighter post iftar sessions.

    Is parking included?

    Members get three free hours of validated parking in the building, plus a reserved bike rack at the entrance.
