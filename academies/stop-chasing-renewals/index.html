How academies stop chasing parents for package renewals

By Ali Al Hashmi, Hashmi Digital, Abu Dhabi

Every academy that sells 8 or 10 session packages knows the feeling: the package runs out, the kid keeps coming, and someone at the front desk has to chase the parent. Here is why it happens and how to stop it.

Why parents forget to renew

Parents aren't avoiding you. They just can't see the balance. Most academies track sessions in a spreadsheet or a notebook at the front desk, so the parent only finds out the package is finished when someone tells them, usually by phone or a WhatsApp message typed by hand.

By then the child has often attended a session or two that wasn't paid for, and now there's an awkward conversation about it.

What it costs

Take a simple example. An academy with 120 swimmers on 8-session packages has roughly 30 renewals due every week. If each one takes a message, a reminder, a bank transfer screenshot and a manual update, that's easily 5 minutes each. That's around 2.5 hours a week spent just on renewals, before counting the sessions that slip through unpaid.

Quick check: how many kids attended a class last month after their package ran out? If you don't know the answer, that's the gap.

The fix: let the balance remind them

  1. Count sessions automatically. When the coach marks a kid present, the package goes down by one. Nobody updates a spreadsheet.
  2. Warn early. When 2 sessions are left, the parent gets a WhatsApp message: "Zayed has 2 sessions left. Renew now?"
  3. Make paying one tap. The message has a button to pay, so renewing takes 10 seconds, not a bank transfer and a screenshot.
  4. Send the receipt in the same chat. The parent sees it went through, and your office sees it live.

Why WhatsApp and not an app

Parents in the UAE already live in WhatsApp. Asking them to download another app means half of them won't. A message from the academy's WhatsApp gets read.
