For academies / Swimming academies

Booking system for swimming academies in Abu Dhabi

By Ali Al Hashmi, Hashmi Digital, Abu Dhabi

Swimming academies run differently from gyms or salons. Kids move through levels, families buy packages, classes are capped at a few swimmers per coach, and most academies use more than one pool. Here is what a booking system needs to handle.

What makes swim academies different

NeedWhy it matters
LevelsKids are booked into a class by level, not just by time. Promotions need to move them up.
Packages8 or 10 sessions that expire. Balances must count down on attendance.
Small class caps4 to 6 swimmers per coach. A full class must stop taking bookings.
Make-upsMissed lessons get rebooked inside the package period.
Several poolsDifferent venues, coaches and timings, one office.

Where generic booking apps fall short

Most booking platforms are built for appointments: one person, one slot. Swimming is weekly, recurring, level-based and package-based. Academies end up working around the software with spreadsheets and WhatsApp anyway.

What we built

Our academy system was designed around how swim academies actually run:

  • Parents book a weekly slot by level and venue, and keep it every week.
  • Coaches mark attendance from the deck, and packages count down on their own.
  • Reminders, make-ups, renewals and receipts all happen inside WhatsApp.
  • The office sees every pool, every class and every balance on one screen.
